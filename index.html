ML from scratch: health data

Regression, classification and a neural network in plain NumPy, tested on diabetes and breast cancer data.

+

Linear regression, logistic regression and a one-hidden-layer neural network written from first principles, with gradient descent and backpropagation coded by hand. Each model is checked against an exact solution or scikit-learn.

The diabetes model reaches a test R² of 0.45, identical to the closed-form answer. The cancer classifiers score 96.5 to 98.2% and catch 40 of 42 malignant tumours. The write-up explains why the largest diabetes weight is misleading (two blood measures are 0.89 correlated) and why the two missed cancers can't be fixed by moving the threshold.

NPMS clinical ward app

Blocks unsafe prescriptions, flags deteriorating patients and keeps a tamper-evident record of every action.

+

A Nurse Patient Management System built around one rule: zero patient harm. Prescriptions are checked against the patient's allergies and current medication before they save. Vital signs are scored with the Modified Early Warning Score so at-risk patients stand out on the ward board.

Every sign-in, observation and prescription is written to an audit log where each entry carries a SHA-256 hash of the one before, so any edit breaks the chain. Database security rules, not just the interface, decide who may prescribe. The interface is available in English and isiZulu.

Group project, CSC312 Software Engineering, team Software Xccelerators. I was the technical lead: I designed the system's structure and led its implementation.

  • Firebase
  • Firestore rules
  • Tailwind
  • Vitest

BBL ConnectBot

A chat front desk that quotes, queues and confirms customers for a local digital services shop.

+

Small shops lose customers at the counter while people wait to ask prices. ConnectBot identifies what the customer needs, quotes from a single server-side price list, and collects a name and WhatsApp number.

When a ticket is complete it is logged to Google Sheets, staff get an email alert and the customer receives a WhatsApp confirmation. Each integration is isolated, so if one fails the others still run and the customer still gets an answer.

Built for IFS317 Information Systems Project Implementation.
